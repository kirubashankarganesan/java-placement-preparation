Encapsulation is the process of wrapping data and methods into a single class and restricting direct access to the data.

Encapsulation na data-va direct-ah access panna mudiyama private ah hide panni, methods moolama controlled access kudukkuradhu.

Encapsulation = private data + getter/setter + controlled access

1. Encapsulation

ATM-la your PIN and account balance are protected.

You cannot directly access or change them.

For example:

PIN → Private
Balance → Private

You can access them only through controlled operations like:

Enter PIN
Check Balance
Withdraw Money

👉 Data-va protect pannuradhu = Encapsulation
